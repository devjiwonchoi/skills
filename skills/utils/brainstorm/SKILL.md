name: brainstorm
description: Always use when brainstorming or creating ideas.
---

The goal is to create ideas through the broadest and deepest relevant research possible.

1. Read the request, context, and prior records.
2. Use [research](../research/SKILL.md) to explore similar, adjacent, new, and previously attempted approaches from many perspectives.
3. Use [first-principle](../first-principle/SKILL.md) to challenge assumptions and create distinct ideas from the findings.
4. Present ideas with their mechanisms, sources, benefits, tradeoffs, and open questions.
