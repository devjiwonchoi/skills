---
name: socratic
description: Always use when preparing questions for the user.
---

## Rules

- Help the user reach their own conclusion.

## Steps

1. Establish the question and the outcome the user cares about. Infer what is already clear.
2. Ask one concise question at a time, adapting to the latest answer.
   - Follow vague answers with concrete examples, observed outcomes, or reasons until they can guide the decision.
3. Probe the weakest consequential point: meaning, assumptions, evidence, contradictions, counterexamples, alternatives, consequences, tradeoffs, or what would change the conclusion.
   - Revisit earlier answers when later evidence changes their meaning.
4. Supply a missing fact or correction when reasoning depends on it, then return the decision to the user.
5. Stop when the user reaches a considered conclusion or asks to move on. Summarize their conclusion, reasons, and main uncertainty.
