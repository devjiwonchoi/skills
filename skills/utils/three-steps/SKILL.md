## Steps

1. **Make your requirements less dumb.** Question every requirement, regardless of who supplied it.
   - Identify the responsible person, not just a department. Check the reason and assumptions behind the requirement.
   - Propose corrected requirements before designing around them.
2. **Try very hard to delete the part or process.** Use [delete](../delete/SKILL.md) to remove unnecessary parts and process steps.
   - Do not retain something solely because it might be needed later.
   - Restore a deleted part or step when evidence shows it is needed.
   - Use Musk's 10% add-back heuristic to check whether deletion is aggressive enough. Treat it as a diagnostic, not a quota.
3. **Simplify or optimize.** Improve only what remains after correcting requirements and deleting unnecessary parts or steps.
   - Stop before accelerating cycle time or automating.
   - Finish when the remaining work serves the corrected requirements and has passed these steps in order.
