## Rules

- Preserve the user's fixed choices and decision boundaries.
- Stop at analysis unless the user asks to plan or execute.

## Steps

1. State the real problem and desired outcome.
2. Separate verified facts and hard constraints from assumptions and convention.
3. Build possible answers from the facts and constraints that remain.
4. Test the result against missing constraints and the strongest conventional alternative. Label uncertainty.
   - Finish when the conclusion follows from stated facts and constraints rather than analogy alone.
