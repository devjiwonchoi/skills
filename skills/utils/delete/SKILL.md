## Steps

1. Briefly check the goal before starting and as work progresses.
2. Ask what each part contributes and what would be lost without it.
3. Omit, remove, or simplify what is unnecessary before adding more.
   - Cut repetition and details that do not help the user understand or decide.
   - For code, trace actual uses and identify the behavior or contract lost by deletion.
   - Remove related code, types, tests, and documentation together.
   - Trace again after deletion to find newly unnecessary structures.
4. Preserve required meaning, behavior, and agreed constraints within authorized scope.
   - Ask before changing approved requirements. Questions alone do not authorize edits.
5. Before finishing, trim again until only what the goal needs remains.
