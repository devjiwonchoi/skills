name: explore
description: Use when the goal or approach is unsettled.
---

The goal is to choose a direction when a request leaves the goal or approach open, then hand off to Align.

## Steps

1. Read the request.
   - If the goal and approach are clear from the request, skip the rest of this skill and follow the `align` skill.
2. Diverge with `brainstorm` into 2-4 distinct directions.
3. Converge on one direction.
   - Compare what each direction gains and gives up, then recommend one.
   - If the choice is a two-way door, easily reversible, take the recommendation as the chosen direction. Do not ask.
   - If the choice is a one-way door, not easily reversible, let the user pick the chosen direction.
4. Save `EXPLORE.md` in the task folder per the `task-recording` skill, then follow the `align` skill.
   - Include the chosen direction, rejected directions with reasons, and open questions.
   - Report a two-way recommendation in Align. Align reads `EXPLORE.md`.
